Frequently Asked Questions

Is the accuracy guaranteed?

No. Accuracy is based on the historical tick sample and the backtest methodology shown in the dashboard. Historical performance does not guarantee future results, and trading always carries risk.

Which markets are supported?

The dashboard includes several Deriv volatility indices. The production application can be extended to load currently available symbols dynamically.

Do I need prior trading experience?

The interface is designed to be beginner-friendly, but users should understand the risks of digit trading before risking money.

Is my Deriv account connected?

No. Market-data signals use Deriv's public WebSocket. digitpredictor does not execute trades or request Deriv account credentials.

Is support available after purchase?

Support can be provided according to your published support policy. Paid access is activated only after the backend verifies the payment on-chain.
